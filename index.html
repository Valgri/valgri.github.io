youtube-pipeline

A personal tool by Valerii Grishko for the YouTube channels he runs: Held Together, Owl Light and Nightline Radio.

It signs in with Google to read the statistics of these channels (views, watch time, audience retention) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. This Google access is read-only. The tool also uploads the author's own short videos to his own Owl Light account on TikTok, where he reviews and publishes each post himself. It is not a public service and is used only by its author.
