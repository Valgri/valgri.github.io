What the tool is

youtube-pipeline is a personal tool by Valerii Grishko. It reads statistics of his YouTube channels and uploads his own short videos to his own social media accounts, including the Owl Light account on TikTok. It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up, and no account other than the author's is connected to it.
